Discover Riverston

Nestled in the breathtaking Matale district at the gateway to Riverston, our bed and breakfast offers an authentic mountain retreat surrounded by cloud-kissed peaks and lush greenery. Wake up to the symphony of birds, breathe in the crisp mountain air, and let the misty mornings refresh your soul. We're perfectly positioned for exploring the legendary Riverston viewpoints, the enchanting Pitawala Pathana grasslands, and the majestic Knuckles Mountain Range - a UNESCO World Heritage site. Whether you're an adventure seeker or simply yearning for peace, our welcoming home provides the ideal base for your mountain escape.
